General Readme - Project Nyx
============================

Overview
--------
Project Nyx is a top-down space RTS with cosmic horror and automation RPG elements.
The player pilots a single hero ship, builds a faction, and reactivates ancient
Sentinel warships that fight autonomously.

Core pillars
------------
- Gardener of War: discover and reboot Sentinel hulks instead of building units.
- Automation loop: mine asteroids, deploy drones, refine ore into energy and ammo.
- Swarm threats: corrupted alien swarms and elder horrors as escalating threats.
- Faction growth: recruit crew and hero characters, grow fleets and trade routes.

Narrative snapshot
------------------
- Act 1: Sol system politics, pirates, and early faction building.
- Act 2: first contact and the broader war against corrupted species.
- Act 3: discovery of Sentinels and the first elder encounter.
- Act 4: origins of the ancient humans and the Sentinel program.
- Act 5: reactivate Sentinels across the galaxy to stop the reaping cycle.

Technical targets
-----------------
- Unity 6 LTS, URP 2D lighting, new Input System.
- Performance for swarms using pooling and spatial partitioning.
- AI state machines for Sentinels and organic movement for Eldritch enemies.
